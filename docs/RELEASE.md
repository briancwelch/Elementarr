Elementarr 0.5.3 makes cards clearer and filters easier to use.

- Movie and TV information appears in darker panels attached to the poster. The Private LAN/version panel also has a darker background; the account/sign-out panel keeps its existing appearance.
- TV completion progress moves below the Sonarr episode count, leaving artwork clear. Episode counts use accent badges, and both affected-history count badges use the warning theme.
- TV completion and notification countdown bars use animated Bootstrap stripes, respecting disabled animations and reduced-motion preferences.
- Included/excluded genres, languages, countries and content certifications use dismissible Bootstrap 6 chips with appropriate icons. Included genres are green and excluded genres are red.
- Languages, countries and certifications support multiple selections, matching any selected value within each filter. Minimum TMDB votes is a slider with a live count and support for existing saved thresholds.

Signed automatic updates remain public and token-free, preserving persistent settings, Plex ownership, collections and watch history.
